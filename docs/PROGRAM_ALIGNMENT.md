# ClaimTrace capability alignment for the 15-program application portfolio

Updated 2026-08-20 from the applicant's 2026-08-16 final 15-program materials. That supplied list is the selection authority for this document; earlier lists are obsolete. Linked university pages are supporting references and can change.

The **official basis** column paraphrases a program page. The **ClaimTrace bridge** is an applicant-authored interpretation of which committed artifact can demonstrate a related capability. It is not an admissions preference stated by a school, and it never turns a synthetic fixture, public-data demonstration, or internal test into client work or real-world impact.

ClaimTrace remains one coherent project: a local-first system that compares versioned data, reruns the claims and decisions built on it, and exports an independently recomputable AuditBundle. The ten cases are method-focused demonstrations of that system—not ten separate research studies.

## Ten-case capability map

| Case | Executed method and decision role | Capabilities demonstrated | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Business operations](../public/cases/business-operations/) | Grouped rank, threshold, and stability rules determine whether spend and service-capacity guidance can be reused. | Business analytics, operations, capacity trade-offs, executive communication | Controlled synthetic regression fixture; no client, revenue, spend, or measured operating impact. |
| [Financial risk](../public/cases/financial-risk/) | Portfolio means, sample-membership change, missingness, and governed thresholds test whether an admission policy remains usable. | Financial risk, model monitoring, threshold governance, decision analysis | Synthetic credit records; no calibrated underwriting model, backtest, or lending outcome. |
| [Population health](../public/cases/population-health/) | Raw-to-summary lineage, recall gates, regional ranks, and constrained options link model evidence to pilot and resource decisions. | AI governance, health analytics, multi-stage lineage, resource allocation | Synthetic model and follow-up records; no clinical validation, patient outcome, or deployment. |
| [Spatial planning](../public/cases/spatial-planning/) | Demand and accessibility ranks are combined with risk and capacity constraints to reconsider a site choice. | Spatial planning, multi-criteria choice, accessibility, sensitivity analysis | Synthetic attribute table; no GIS network, fieldwork, or regeneration-effect estimate. |
| [World Bank life expectancy](../public/cases/world-bank-life-expectancy/) | Cross-year ranks, thresholds, and selected-country mean stability test publication statements. | Population indicators, public-data provenance, cross-country comparison, reproducibility | Selected descriptive indicators; not a causal, representative, or country-performance study. |
| [USDOT transit operations](../public/cases/usdot-transit-operations/) | Derived ridership per vehicle-revenue hour, threshold review, and period stability test whether an operations brief can be reused. | Operations analytics, public infrastructure, source adapters, human review | Selected public rows; not causal efficiency, service quality, or client work. |
| [U.S. Treasury yield curve](../public/cases/us-treasury-yield-curve/) | Curve-shape ranks, a short-end threshold, and period-mean stability test fixed-income statements and downstream action. | Fixed income, risk communication, financial provenance, decision discipline | Descriptive observations; not trading, valuation, allocation, or return evidence. |
| [CFPB credit-card complaints](../public/cases/cfpb-credit-card-complaints/) | Issue ranks, matched shares, denominator tracking, and record-volume stability test a consumer-friction brief. | Consumer finance, behavioral context, sampling limits, policy communication | Complaints are not representative incidence, adjudicated findings, or intervention effects. |
| [CDC PLACES depression estimates](../public/cases/cdc-places-depression/) | Point-estimate ranks and stability are combined with a 95% interval-aware threshold gate. | Health data, statistical uncertainty, surveillance, methods review | Model-based county estimates; not diagnosis, epidemiology, causal intervention, or clinical advice. |
| [ONS housing affordability](../public/cases/ons-housing-affordability/) | Local-authority ranks, affordability thresholds, and period stability test whether a planning-context note remains valid. | Planning evidence, housing affordability, official statistics, place-based decisions | Attribute-table context only; no GIS geometry, spatial econometrics, fieldwork, or impact evaluation. |

## Recommended application use

1. Introduce ClaimTrace once: the versioned-evidence problem, the local-first architecture, individual contribution, and verification boundary.
2. Select one primary case for the target program; add a second only when it demonstrates a genuinely different capability.
3. State the exact committed audit result, then state the downstream decision consequence separately.
4. Name the strongest relevant limitation before proposing future work.
5. Present the six public-data audits as public-data demonstrations and the four synthetic cases as controlled stress fixtures. Neither category is client impact.

## Remaining gaps that documentation cannot honestly erase

- **Behavioral science:** a real randomized or credible quasi-experimental intervention with a preregistered estimand and effect analysis would materially strengthen Penn MBDS and HKS evidence.
- **Health informatics:** terminology/ontology, interoperability, clinical workflow, access control, and domain validation would materially strengthen Yale Health Informatics evidence.
- **Statistics and health research:** one interval-aware threshold is now executable, but formal estimation, model validation, and a larger inferential study remain absent.
- **Finance:** a reproducible out-of-sample allocation or risk-model study with leakage, costs, and benchmark controls would strengthen Yale Asset Management and Columbia FinTech.
- **Planning:** GIS geometry, network accessibility, spatial dependence, scenario comparison, and stakeholder or implementation evidence would strengthen Cambridge PGR.
- **External validity:** independent domain review, authenticated governance, a production-grade hosted workflow, and verified real-user outcomes require external conditions and must not be simulated in repository copy.
